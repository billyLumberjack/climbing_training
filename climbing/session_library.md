# Climbing Session Library

Canonical catalogue of the ONLY climbing session types the Climbing
Agent is allowed to prescribe. Every type below was actually used in
the athlete's own schede (`sessions/*Volumi allenamento.xlsx`) across
the mesocycles from `2023_03` to `2025_02`. Do not invent new types.

Definitions come from the schede glossaries (`all_sheets/*Glossario`)
where available; the rest are derived from the parameters actually
logged in those schede. Typical ranges below are indicative anchors
taken from real sessions — scale them to the week's position in the
progression, not as fixed prescriptions.

Names are kept in the athlete's original Italian (that is how he
recognises them); an English gloss follows each.

---

## Boulder-based

### Blocchi project — "project bouldering / limit boulders"
Supra-maximal boulders: pick problems hard enough that you must try
the individual moves several times to do them. Mark them and keep
working the same singles across sessions/weeks, aiming to link and
send in the medium/long term. Max strength / limit recruitment.
- Session length: `Fino a 45'–100'` (as a max duration, not sets).
- Rest: free / `Almeno 2'–3'` between good attempts.
- Movements: single hard moves (≈ 4–8).
- Typical RPE: 7–9.

### Blocchi ripetuti — "repeated boulders"
Choose a boulder you can flash or send in a few tries; do the
prescribed number of reps consecutively before resting, then repeat
for the prescribed sets. Power-endurance on short problems.
- Sets: 4–8. Reps/set: 2–3.
- Rest: ≈ `45"–1'15" between reps | 5–7' between sets`.
- Typical RPE: 6–8.

### Blocchi flash (a.k.a. "Blocchi flash - No fail") — "flash boulders, no falls"
Flash-level boulders climbed clean, no failing. Low-stress volume at
a controllable grade; keeps quality high and skin/CNS fresh.
- Session length: `Min 20'–30'` / up to `Fino a 90'` in volume weeks.
- Rest: `Min 2'30"–3'`.
- Typical RPE: ~5.

### Blocchi lenti — "slow boulders"
Boulders climbed slowly with continuous tension, at least 3–4" per
move; many reps per problem. Strength/tension and body control.
Light execution cheating accepted; if you speed the moves up, drop
the grade.
- Movements: 6–12. Reps: 10–12 (≥ 3–4" per move).
- Sets: 2–6. Rest: `Almeno 5'–7'`.
- Typical RPE: 7.5–8.

### Blocchi tecnici — "technical boulders"
Technique-focused blocks: heel hooks, drop-knees/lolotte, toe hooks,
foot precision and translations, hip opening to stay close to the
wall, optimising position to reduce effort even on easy moves.
Low intensity, zero pump. Best in the boulder gym on
slabs/coordination problems.
- Sets: 2–6. Session length: `Fino a 40'–70'`.
- Rest: `Max 3'` (short, skill practice) or `Almeno 6'` when tension is high.

### Blocchi Campus — "campus boulders"
Campus-style power blocks. Explosive, few moves, full recovery.
Do not accelerate the movements too much or the stimulus is lost.
- Movements: 4–12. Sets: 2–5.
- Rest: `Fino a 4'–6'`.
- Contact strength / power.

## Circuit-based

### Circuiti — "boulder circuits"
Longer linked circuits for anaerobic endurance / lactic capacity.
- Movements: 12–36 (scales with the endurance target).
- Sets: 2–8. Rest: `7–9'`.
- Typical RPE: 7–9.

### Circuiti ripetuti — "repeated circuits"
A circuit repeated consecutively for reps before the set rest.
Power-endurance bridge between Blocchi ripetuti and full Circuiti.
- Movements: ~12. Reps/set: 2–3. Sets: 3–8.
- Rest: ≈ `45"–1'15" between reps | 6–7' between sets`.
- RPE cap style: `Max RPE 7–9`.

### Circuiti rest attivo — "active-rest circuits"
Circuits with very short active rest between reps — continuous
tension / capillarisation and aerobic-power work.
- Movements: 12–18. Reps: 2–3. Sets: 2–6.
- Rest: `15"–30" between reps | 9'+ between sets`.

## Aerobic / build-up

### Aero — "aerobic intervals"
On/off aerobic climbing intervals for base endurance and continuity.
- Format: e.g. `4' ON / 2–3' OFF`, `5' ON / 2' OFF`.
- Reps: 2–3.
- Typical RPE: 6–9 depending on block.

### Aufbau — "build-up / decreasing sets"
Pyramidal build-up on the Moonboard: decreasing sets (e.g. 10–13 →
8 moves). Strength-endurance on board with capped intensity.
- Movements: 10–15. Reps: 3–7. Sets: 1–3.
- Rest: ≈ `1'40"–2' between reps | 8–10' between sets`.
- Typical RPE: capped at RPE 7–8 (logged up to 8.5).

## Routes (lead) — rope gym is vertical only; no steep PE on routes

### Vie flash — "flash routes"
On-sight / flash mileage on routes. Reading, repertoire, pump
management at a comfortable grade.
- Routes: 3–6. Rest: free ("A piacere").

### Vie ripetute — "repeated routes"
Redpoint laps: repeat the same route for reps to build sostenuta /
PE on route. TUT-oriented.
- Reps: 2 per route. Sets: 2–5. Rest: free.

### Vie red point — "redpoint routes"
Projecting / redpointing a hard route across attempts.
- Attempts: up to 3–5. Rest: free.
- Typical RPE: ~8.5.

## Facility fit (recap)

- **Moonboard 2024** — favourite; prefer for Blocchi project, Blocchi
  ripetuti, Blocchi lenti, Blocchi Campus, Aufbau, Circuiti (board).
- **Boulder gym** — limited overhang; best for Blocchi tecnici,
  Blocchi flash, coordinative work.
- **Rope gym** — vertical / mildly overhanging only; supports Vie
  flash / Vie ripetute / Vie red point and vertical PE, NOT steep
  route endurance.
