# Meso 6 — 2026_late_autumn_raise

**Window:** Mon 2026-10-26 → Sun 2026-12-27 (9 weeks)
**Goal:** **Raise the finger-strength & pulling-strength ceiling** over winter, then convert it to power — a dedicated max-strength/power block of the kind the autumn plan explicitly deferred ("a true pull peak belongs in a dedicated winter block"). The athlete is fundamentally an endurance/on-sight climber (just on-sighted a 7b nemesis, "Claudia procura", end of Meso 5); this block deliberately flips the emphasis to the quality that erosion and the Test-4 read flagged as the weak link — maximum strength — on the principle that a higher strength ceiling structurally lifts the endurance floor (any submaximal climbing load becomes a smaller % of max).
**Performance window:** **W9 (Sat–Sun Dec 26–27)** — the Christmas break. **Expression = ROUTE / REDPOINT** (confirmed 2026-10-27): peak on a hard lead redpoint (indoor rope gym, or an outdoor sport route if conditions allow). The block keeps its max-strength base (W2–W5) but the conversion phase (W6–W8) tilts toward **route-specific power-endurance** — sustained lead, Vie ripetute / sostenuta, and board circuits for the steep PE the vertical rope gym can't provide — feeding redpoint attempts in W9. Moonboard (40°) carries the power/strength stimulus; rope gym (vertical) carries route volume; steep power-endurance lives on board circuits. NB: this is a redpoint (rehearsed) peak, not on-sight — distinct from Meso-5's goal.
**No fixed event beyond Christmas.**

**Constraints honored:** persistent WFH pattern (Mon/Tue/Fri home gym; Wed/Thu office → train 18:00+; Sat/Sun climbing). Weekend = **two climbing days, one kept optional** (per athlete). Facilities: Moonboard 2024 (primary, steep, preferred), boulder gym (limited overhangs), rope gym (vertical only — no steep route PE). Home-gym equipment only for home sessions.

**ACTIVE LIMITATION — forearm flexor soreness (reported Mon 2026-10-26).** Coming straight off the Meso-5 performance peak, the athlete reports the forearms "a bit still painful." This gates the start of the block (see §0 and the test note). Treat as mild post-peak muscular overuse **unless** it localizes to a tendon or the base of a finger — then it is handled as an injury, not trained through (see §0).

**Framework:** **block periodization — accumulation → power conversion → taper/peak** (KB: chapter_8 designing-the-program; chapter_5 strength; chapter_7 climbing-specific exercises; Saeterbakken 2024 resistance-training review; Hermans 2022 10-week hangboard). Classic climbing off-season logic: build the maximum-strength foundation first, then convert it to RFD/power nearer the window, maintaining (not developing) the endurance base throughout.

## 0. Pre-block gate — forearm recovery + Test 5 (W1)

**W1 is a recovery/transition week, not a training week.** KB injury guidance (chapter_11 injuries; chapter_10 recovery) for post-peak flexor soreness:

- **Deload finger loading for ~1 week minimum.** Muscular soreness (DOMS) clears in ~2–5 days; tendon tissue is slower. No MaxHangs, no campus, no hard bouldering until the forearms are quiet.
- **Red-flag triage (athlete self-check):** soreness only, no pain when gripping, none at rest → mild muscular overuse, proceed with the recovery week. **But** sharp/localized pain on the palm side of a finger or at the finger base, pain that is asymptomatic at rest but bites on gripping, any "pop", or pain that *worsens with rest* → stop, treat as flexor tendinosis / A2-pulley and seek assessment; the Christmas peak becomes secondary to not converting mild overuse into frank tendinosis.
- **"Pain-free" ≠ "cleared to max-load."** Re-enter finger loading sub-maximally (W2 is deliberately a ramp, not a max week) and cut back at the first sign of pain. 48 h minimum between finger-loading sessions, every week of the block.

**Test 5 (full fitness battery)** — due now (6+ wks since Test 4 on 2026-09-11, at a mesocycle boundary) and it should **open this block to calibrate every load in it**. But it is a max test → run it **only on a genuinely fresh, pain-free day**: late W1 (≈ Fri Oct 30–Sun Nov 1) if the forearms have settled, otherwise slip it into early W2. Record into `testing/fitness-test-protocol.md` as the Test 5 column (BW same clothed+shoes denominator; MVC7, CF repeaters with right-hand bias, finger deadlift, 3RM pull + AMRAP, RFD if load cell). **Do not start Phase-1 accumulation loads until Test 5 is in.**

Warm up thoroughly (progressive dead-hangs) every time. 48 h minimum between finger sessions.
- **PHYSICAL (pull-rebuild spine + antagonist/wrist + core)** — 2 sessions/wk on home days (Mon/Fri), paired after the hangboard on shared days. **Weighted-pull max-strength progression is the backbone**; wrist-extension + pronator antagonist and hip/ankle mobility go INSIDE the session (recurring lesson: bolt-on mini-blocks die). Power phase: add **campus-board / complex-training power work** (max 2 power sessions/wk, brief, after warm-up when freshest, 3–5′ between efforts) and dynamic/limit Moonboard; a midweek (Wed/Thu eve) power session appears here. ## 3. Rationale

- **W1 (recovery + test):** Non-negotiable given the sore flexors. No training hangs; one light non-finger physical session (mobility, antagonist/wrist, push, core) and at most one easy big-hold movement session. **Test 5** when fresh and pain-free (late W1, else early W2) — it opens and calibrates the block. This also *is* the required mesocycle-boundary fitness test, so the recovery week costs us nothing programmatically.
- **W2 (accumulation ramp-in):** Re-enter finger loading **sub-maximally** per the KB post-deload rule — MaxHangs at ~80–90% of test loads, movement-quality first; the pull thread restarts. Hard bouldering kept moderate this week. Protects the just-recovered flexors.
- **W3–W4 (accumulation / max-strength peak):** The strength core. MaxHangs progress toward a MVC7 PR (sets 3→4→5 across the phase, 3′ rest, failure-with-margin loads); weighted-pull max-strength drives the 3RM rebuild; hard Moonboard limit bouldering is the on-wall strength stimulus. **W4 may run a short HIT/hypergravity sub-block** if the forearms are robust. Max strength never on consecutive days; 3–5′ rest on all max work. Endurance *maintained* via one submax route/wk + optional light aerobic on off days.
- **W5 (deload micro + bridge):** Planned unload (HB 1 / PT 1 / C 2, intensity held, volume cut ~40%) to consolidate the accumulation gains and arrive fresh into the power phase — a strength block needs this hinge or RFD conversion happens on a fatigued CNS.
- **W6–W7 (power conversion):** Convert the ceiling to RFD/power — the quality that separates strong-but-slow from powerful. **Campus board + complex training** (pair a max effort with an explosive one, cycled ~2 weeks), dynamic/limit Moonboard, grip-RFD hangs (short maximal pulls). KB evidence: 4–5 wk of this reliably yields +23–33% RFD once a strength base exists. **IntHangs 1–2×/wk** inserted here specifically to stop CF eroding during the power emphasis (right-hand bias). Power work is brief, fresh, never on consecutive days, max 2/wk — high power volume is an injury recipe.
- **W8 (peak build → taper start):** Last sharp power touches, then begin cutting volume ~40–50% while holding intensity. Pull/hangboard to maintenance.
- **W9 (taper + window):** 4–7 days light before the Christmas weekend; one brief early-week primer (hangboard light + one short power touch) to keep neural patterns, then the **Sat 26–Sun 27 performance window** — express the strength/power on hard Moonboard benchmarks / limit boulders (outdoor boulder if conditions). Fri 25 (Christmas Day) = rest/family. Freshness beats every primer (the Meso-5 "reminiscence effect" lesson).
- **RPE discipline (recurring lesson):** prescribed RPE/load is a ceiling, not a floor — especially re-entering after the sore forearms and on power work where quality > volume.
