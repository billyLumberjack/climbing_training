## Test-driven priorities (from the 2026-09-11 battery — Test 4, incl. first valid CF)

Read these as the "why" behind the weekly emphasis.

1. **CF is the #1 lever for this goal.** CF%BW explains ~61% of sport-climbing performance variance (Giles 2021, KB). First valid CF baseline: **L 18.67 kg (28.7% BW) / R 15.85 kg (24.4% BW)** — solid-intermediate with clear headroom. Raising CF%BW is the single highest-yield physiological target for on-sight 7a+. → **CF-zone 7:3 repeaters are the spine of the hangboard block; route AE/pump work is the spine of climbing.**
2. **Right-hand CF deficit (~15% below left, above the 10% flag).** The right hand is the endurance-floor limiter on sustained routes (bigger W′/RFD = power-biased; lower CF = lower aerobic floor). → **Right-hand bias / unilateral emphasis in the CF repeaters** to close the gap.
3. **MVC7 maintained** (~147–150% BW across T3→T4) — fingers held max strength through three non-strength mesos. → Keep a **max-hang maintenance+light-progression thread**; no PR chasing on max hangs.
4. **Pull strength eroded** (3RM pull-up 29→22.5→24.5→**20 kg**, lowest ever). Not the on-sight limiter, but low enough to arrest. → **Modest pull-rebuild thread** (resume weighted pull-ups toward the deferred +9 kg PR) front-loaded in W1/W3, maintenance thereafter. This is a thread, **not** a strength peak — a true pull peak belongs in a dedicated winter block.
5. **Finger deadlift up** (46.3→48 kg, 73% BW) — the one finger quality that improved; keep it ticking at maintenance, retest next round.
6. **W′ is large on the right** (1256 vs 762 kg·s) — great for boulder cruxes, but W′ matters far more for bouldering (34% variance) than sport (7%); it's not the bottleneck here. Don't over-train burst capacity at the expense of CF.

Next test due ~mid-to-late Nov (6–8 wk), after this block. Keep the clothed+shoes weigh-in consistent (true BW ~63 kg; the %BW figures above are on the 65 kg denominator, so slightly conservative).

## 3. Rationale

- **W1 (re-entry + rebuild):** First structured week after the test. Hangboard returns to a progressive max-hang thread (rebuild toward the Meso 2 references 18.5 kg semiarcuata / 23.5 kg svase) plus the first CF repeaters; physical re-starts the pull thread. Climbing kept to 2 (one midweek, one light-moderate weekend day) — the priority is **arriving fresh to the trip**, not stacking two big outdoor weekends back-to-back. The W1 weekend day is the lower end / conditions-dependent.
- **W2 (trip):** Three outdoor days (Fri–Sun) are on-sight mileage and movement-repertoire gold — exactly the random-practice the goal needs — and simultaneously a natural deload from structured S&C (travel). Home training drops to a single light hangboard + light mobility early week so fingers/skin are fresh for the rock. Trip climbing = on-sight/flash mileage with the pre-climb reading ritual, RPE-managed (it's mileage, not projecting).
- **W3 (bridge):** First 1–2 days recover from three big climbing days. Then resume strength/power (last progressive push on pull + max-hang) while the AE/CF thread steps up — hangboard repeaters now primary, right-hand biased; climbing adds the first route-AE session. Climbing 2–3 by recovery state.
- **W4 (AE/CF peak):** Volume peak, tilted to pump management. Hangboard CF repeaters at full dose (CF-zone loads, right-hand emphasis) + max-hang maintenance; physical to maintenance; climbing to 3 — midweek route intervals (1:1–1:2 work:rest, 120–180 s TUT carried from Meso 3/4) and weekend outdoor on-sight/sostenuta mileage. Climbing-specific muscular endurance (hard+easy lead within a session) is the most on-sight-transferable method (KB).
- **W5 (integration):** Hold intensity, volume begins easing. Climbing shifts toward on-sight simulation on unseen routes + the reading ritual as the centrepiece; CF repeaters maintained. This is where the endurance base meets the skill.
- **W6 (taper + window):** Reduced volume, intensity held; hangboard/physical light and early-week only; climbing down to 2 with the weekend as the **on-sight performance window**. Leverages the KB "reminiscence effect" — movement feel and route-reading intuition peak after a brief unloading.
- **RPE discipline (recurring lesson):** prescribed RPE is a ceiling, not a floor — especially on the trip and on skill/on-sight work, where overshooting mileage inhibits the acquisition this block is for.
